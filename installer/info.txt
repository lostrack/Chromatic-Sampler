Chromatic Sampler - what gets installed where

VST3 plugin (always)
  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3$name.vst3

Standalone app (optional)
  C:\Program Files\Seleven Audio$name$name.exe
  Start Menu shortcut, desktop shortcut if you tick it.

Nothing else. No background services, no extra downloads.
Uninstall from Settings > Apps.

Close your DAW before you install, then rescan plugins.
